Rule 2A. Conditions for exemption of house rent allowance.
(1) For the purposes of clause (13A) of section 10, the prescribed conditions shall be the following, namely:—
(i) the assessee actually incurs expenditure on payment of rent (by whatever name called) in respect of residential accommodation occupied by him;
(ii) the expenditure referred to in clause (i) is in excess of ten per cent of the amount of salary due to the assessee in respect of the relevant period; and
(iii) the house rent allowance is actually received by the assessee in respect of the relevant period.

(2) The amount which shall be exempted under clause (13A) of section 10 shall be the least of the following amounts, namely:—
(i) the house rent allowance actually received by the assessee in respect of the relevant period;
(ii) the expenditure incurred by the assessee on payment of rent in respect of residential accommodation occupied by him, as reduced by ten per cent of the salary due to the assessee in respect of the relevant period; and
(iii) an amount equal to fifty per cent of the salary due to the assessee in respect of the relevant period, where the place of residence is situated at Bombay, Calcutta, Delhi or Madras, and forty per cent of the salary due to the assessee in respect of the relevant period, where the place of residence is situated at any other place.

(3) For the purposes of this rule, "salary" includes dearness allowance, if the terms of employment so provide, but excludes all other allowances and perquisites.
